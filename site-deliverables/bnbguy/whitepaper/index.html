Treasury and liquidity mechanics

There is no guaranteed revenue. Sponsor income, platform earnings or other receipts should only be described as received when supported by records. Holdings and unrealised appreciation are not the same as cash income.

The project’s stated approach, when funds are available, is to add liquidity, burn the LP tokens received and publish the transactions. Reports should distinguish funds added, LP received, LP burned and any separate CAKE or BNBGUY token burns. There is no fixed burn schedule or guaranteed allocation of future receipts.
