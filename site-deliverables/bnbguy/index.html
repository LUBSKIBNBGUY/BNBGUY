How it works

There is no guaranteed revenue. When treasury funds are available, the stated approach is to add liquidity, burn the LP tokens received and publish the transactions. Reports should distinguish funds added, LP received, LP burned and any separate CAKE or BNBGUY token burns. There is no fixed burn schedule and no guaranteed allocation of future receipts (November 2026 whitepaper).

  1. Funds availableOnly if funds exist. Holdings are not cash income.
